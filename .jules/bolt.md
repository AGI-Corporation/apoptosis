## 2026-05-06 - Stan Performance Optimization: Algebraic Simplification and Loop Hoisting
**Learning:** Significant performance gains in Stan can be achieved by algebraically simplifying analytic ODE solutions and hoisting parameter transformations (like `exp()`) out of observation-level loops. Replacing multiple `exp()` calls with a consolidated expression and using `expm1()` for stability when $t$ is small reduced execution time by ~1.7x. Additionally, hoisting parameters into local blocks `{}` in `transformed parameters` prevents them from being saved to the output, saving disk space and reducing I/O without sacrificing speed.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to simplify analytic expressions and hoist invariant calculations out of loops. Use local blocks in Stan to keep the output namespace clean of intermediate optimized parameters.
