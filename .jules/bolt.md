## 2026-04-04 - Stan Model Optimization and Compatibility
**Learning:** Algebraic simplification of analytic ODE solutions significantly improves sampling speed in Stan. By reducing the number of `exp()` calls in the core `yt` function from ~6 to 1-3, and lifting parameter transformations (like `exp(log_kq)`) out of the observation loop into the `transformed parameters` block, we achieved a ~1.6x speedup. Additionally, modern Stan (2.33+) requires the `array[]` syntax, and `cmdstanpy` 1.2.0+ is necessary to handle hyphenated flags like `--include-paths` correctly.
**Action:** Always check for redundant `exp()` or `log()` calls in Stan 'transformed parameters' blocks and observation loops. Hoist parameter-level calculations out of $O(N)$ loops and use `fmin`/`fmax` for vectorized thresholding where possible. Ensure numerical stability by adding a small floor (e.g., `1e-9`) to return values used in log-likelihoods.
